## 2. Team Reflection & Discussion Highlights

### What Went Well
- **Solid Technical Foundation:** Scaffolded Next.js App Router with TypeScript strict mode, Turbopack, and Tailwind v4 `@theme` single-sourced tokens.
- **Unified Motion & Scroll Engine:** GSAP 3.12 and Lenis 1.x integrated on a single GSAP ticker, avoiding scroll jitter and respecting `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Accessibility Floor Established Early:** Contrast ratios (≥ 4.5:1), keyboard focus rings (≥ 2px), and aria attributes verified on primitives before section building.

### Key Concerns & Challenges Surfaced by Project Lead
- **Setup Time:** Infrastructure and setup took ~1 hour of automated processing. Accepted as standard for greenfield setup phase, but requires tight execution going forward.
- **Design & Visual Completion (Primary Concern):** Prajwal rightly highlighted that Epic 1 is only the underlying foundation/primitives, and the actual visually striking sections (Hero, Services, Projects, About, Skills, Experience) are not built yet. The design execution in Epic 2 is critical to achieving the intended Cyberpunk Y2K glitch aesthetic.
